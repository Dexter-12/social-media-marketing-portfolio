# Content Strategy & Planning

This guide outlines the framework for developing comprehensive social media content strategies.

## Content Strategy Framework

### 1. Audience Analysis

#### Target Audience Profile
- **Demographics:** Age, gender, location, income level
- **Psychographics:** Interests, values, lifestyle, pain points
- **Online Behavior:** Platforms used, content preferences, peak activity times
- **Needs & Goals:** What problems do they want solved?

#### Buyer Personas
Create detailed personas representing your ideal audience segments:
- Name and background
- Goals and challenges
- Preferred content types
- Decision-making factors

### 2. Platform Selection

| Platform | Best For | Audience Type | Content Style |
|----------|----------|---------------|----------------|
| **Instagram** | Visual storytelling, lifestyle | 18-45, visual-focused | High-quality images, Reels, Stories |
| **TikTok** | Trending, entertainment, trends | 13-30, entertainment | Short-form video, trends, trends |
| **Facebook** | Community building, older demographics | 25-65, community-focused | Mix of content, longer-form |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B, professional content | Professionals, businesses | Articles, industry insights, thought leadership |
| **YouTube** | Long-form video, tutorials | All ages | In-depth videos, tutorials, vlogs |
| **Twitter/X** | News, real-time engagement, discussions | News-focused, tech-savvy | Quick updates, conversations, threads |
| **Pinterest** | Visual discovery, DIY, lifestyle | 25-55, female-leaning | High-quality pins, infographics, inspiration |

### 3. Content Pillars

Define 3-5 main content themes that align with your brand:

**Example Content Pillars:**
1. **Educational** - Tips, tutorials, how-tos
2. **Inspirational** - Motivational content, success stories
3. **Entertaining** - Memes, behind-the-scenes, fun content
4. **Promotional** - Product/service highlights, offers
5. **Community** - User-generated content, engagement

### 5. Content Types & Formats

#### Visual Content
- Square images (1:1)
- Vertical video (9:16)
- Landscape video (16:9)
- Carousels (multiple images)
- Infographics
- Quotes and text overlays

#### Video Content
- Reels (15-60 seconds)
- TikToks (15-60 seconds)
- YouTube videos (5+ minutes)
- Live streams
- Testimonials
- Tutorials
- Behind-the-scenes

#### Text Content
- Captions
- Hashtags
- Threads (Twitter/Meta)
- Blog posts
- Carousels with text
- Stories with text

#### Interactive Content
- Polls
- Quizzes
- Surveys
- Q&As
- Challenges
- Contests
- Giveaways

### 6. Hashtag Strategy

#### Hashtag Categories
- **Branded Hashtags** - Unique to your brand (#YourBrandName)
- **Industry Hashtags** - Topic-specific (#SocialMedia, #DigitalMarketing)
- **Trending Hashtags** - Current trends relevant to your content
- **Niche Hashtags** - Specific to your audience (#SocialMediaTips)

#### Hashtag Guidelines
- Instagram: 15-30 hashtags per post
- TikTok: 3-5 hashtags per video
- Twitter: 1-2 hashtags per tweet
- LinkedIn: 3-5 hashtags per post
- Research hashtags in your niche regularly

### 7. Engagement Strategy

#### Community Management
- Respond to comments within 2 hours
- Reply to DMs promptly
- Engage with audience content
- Foster conversations, not just broadcasting

#### Engagement Tactics
- Ask questions in captions
- Create polls and quizzes
- Respond to trending topics
- Collaborate with other creators
- Host Q&A sessions
- Share user-generated content

### 8. Posting Schedule

#### Optimal Posting Times by Platform
**Instagram:** Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 3 PM
**TikTok:** 6 AM - 10 AM, 7 PM - 11 PM
**Facebook:** Tuesday-Thursday, 1 PM - 3 PM
**LinkedIn:** Tuesday-Wednesday, 8 AM - 10 AM
**Twitter:** Monday-Thursday, 8 AM - 10 AM
**YouTube:** Thursday-Friday, 5 PM

*Note: Optimal times vary by audience. Use platform analytics to determine best times for YOUR specific audience.*

### 9. Performance Metrics to Track

#### Key KPIs
- **Reach** - Total people who see your content
- **Impressions** - Number of times content is displayed
- **Engagement Rate** - (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Impressions × 100
- **Follower Growth** - New followers per week/month
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** - Clicks / Impressions × 100
- **Conversion Rate** - (Conversions / Total Clicks) × 100
- **ROI** - (Revenue - Cost) / Cost × 100

### 10. Monthly Review & Optimization

**Questions to Ask:**
- Which content types performed best?
- What times generated highest engagement?
- Which platforms drove the most conversions?
- What audience insights did we gain?
- What should we do differently next month?

## Content Creation Checklist

Before posting, verify:
- ✓ Content aligns with brand voice and values
- ✓ Visuals are high-quality and on-brand
- ✓ Copy is proofread and error-free
- ✓ Hashtags are relevant and current
- ✓ CTA is clear (what do you want the audience to do?)
- ✓ Posting time is optimal for target audience
- ✓ Links are working (if applicable)
- ✓ Content complies with platform guidelines
- ✓ Image dimensions are correct for platform
- ✓ Alt text is added for accessibility
