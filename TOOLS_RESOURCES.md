# Tools & Resources Guide

A comprehensive reference for social media marketing tools, platforms, and resources.

## Content Creation Tools

### Design
- **Canva Pro** - Graphic design and template creation
- **Adobe Creative Suite** - Professional design tools (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro)
- **Figma** - Collaborative design platform
- **GIMP** - Free open-source image editor

### Video Editing
- **Adobe Premiere Pro** - Professional video editing
- **Final Cut Pro** - Advanced video editing (Mac)
- **DaVinci Resolve** - Professional color grading and editing
- **CapCut** - User-friendly mobile and desktop video editor

### Writing & Copywriting
- **Grammarly** - Writing assistance and proofreading
- **Hemingway Editor** - Simplify writing style
- **ChatGPT/Claude** - Content ideation and draft writing

## Social Media Management Platforms

| Platform | Best For | Cost |
|----------|----------|------|
| **Hootsuite** | Multi-platform scheduling | Free/$49+/month |
| **Buffer** | Simple scheduling | Free/$15+/month |
| **Sprout Social** | Enterprise management | $249+/month |
| **Meta Business Suite** | Facebook/Instagram | Free |
| **Later** | Visual planning | Free/$25+/month |
| **TweetDeck** | Twitter management | Free |

## Analytics & Reporting Tools

- **Google Analytics** - Website traffic and conversion tracking
- **Meta Analytics** (Facebook/Instagram Insights) - Platform-native analytics
- **Brandwatch** - Social listening and sentiment analysis
- **Hootsuite Analytics** - Multi-platform reporting
- **Sprout Social Analytics** - Advanced reporting and ROI tracking
- **Tableau** - Data visualization and dashboards

## Research & Competitor Analysis

- **Semrush** - SEO and social media analytics
- **Ahrefs** - Competitor analysis
- **Social Blade** - YouTube and social media statistics
- **BuzzSumo** - Content and influencer research
- **Google Trends** - Trending topics and keywords

## Influencer & Partnership Tools
